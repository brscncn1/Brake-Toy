Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual direction concept for BRAKE TOY, a real 3D Unity mobile game. This is a target gameplay view, not a promotional poster.
Primary request: A toy-scale red racing car drives on top of a large wooden play table INSIDE a clearly recognizable cozy child's bedroom. The player must visibly understand both the tabletop playground and the room surrounding it.
Composition: one portrait 9:16 image, high rear three-quarter perspective gameplay camera, looking forward over the red toy car. The car is near horizontal center and 25 percent up from the bottom, around 10 percent of image width, front pointing away from viewer. Broad continuous honey-colored wooden tabletop occupies foreground and middle 65 percent. At the far sides and upper 35 percent show the surrounding bedroom in believable 3D perspective: room floor below desk height, a table edge and leg at one side, a warm sunlit window, soft curtains, a small toy shelf, a cozy bed and rug. A long table stretches forward; no cliff or terminal edge directly across the driving route.
Gameplay: A wide unmarked tabletop with free left-right driving and a clearly readable open route around a few giant relative-to-car household objects: a thick colorful book, a three-block plastic toy tower, a pink eraser, and a tennis ball farther ahead. Objects are laid out naturally on the table rather than in traffic lanes. Keep the main route visually clear. Small decorative pencil cup and notebook can sit well outside the driving area.
Style: polished but achievable mobile stylized low-poly / soft 3D game art, rounded toy plastic, charming chunky car silhouette with four visible black rubber wheels, subtle wood grain, simplified bedroom geometry, soft contact shadows, warm afternoon light, gentle ambient occlusion, pleasing colorful materials. Clean readable assets, moderate geometry, not photorealistic and not a cinematic movie still. The room is legible, only mildly softened with distance.
Interface: minimal restrained gameplay UI, small top left distance '248 m', small top right pause symbol, no other text.
Constraints: genuine 3D game-render appearance, deep room context is essential. No roads, no asphalt, no lane lines, no three-lane runner layout, no racing circuit, no floating isolated platform in a void, no human characters, no giant title covering the room, no phone device frame, no watermark.
